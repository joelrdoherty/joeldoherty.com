Accessibility

Software and websites should work for everyone.

Joel Doherty is committed to making joeldoherty.com usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology. The site is designed toward WCAG 2.1 Level AA with semantic structure, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, responsive text and high-contrast content.

Need help?

If something on this site is difficult to use, email heyjoel@joeldoherty.com. Include the page and what you were trying to do, and I will work to provide the information or fix the issue.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is an ongoing practice. New pages and product updates are reviewed as they are added.
