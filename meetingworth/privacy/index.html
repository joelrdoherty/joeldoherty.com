MeetingWorth

Privacy policy.

Effective September 16, 2026

MeetingWorth is designed so your meeting and compensation information stays on your device. The app has no account system, backend, analytics, advertising SDKs or external APIs.

Data storage

People, salary information, role presets, meetings, estimates and settings are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage technologies. Joel Doherty does not receive or have access to that data.

Data collection

MeetingWorth does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It does not track your activity across apps or websites.

Purchases

Optional MeetingWorth Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may process purchase and transaction information under its own privacy policy. MeetingWorth receives only the entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features.

Sharing

You can choose to share a meeting summary or Team Cost Card through the standard iOS share sheet. Shared Team Cost Cards contain aggregate meeting totals and do not include individual names, roles, salaries or compensation rates. You control where shared content is sent.

Data deletion

You can delete saved people and meetings in the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data from your device, subject to Apple's device backup and restoration behavior.

Children

MeetingWorth is a business productivity utility and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to heyjoel@joeldoherty.com.
