Private by architecture

Your salary isn't our business.

MeetingWorth doesn't have an account system. It doesn't upload meeting information, track what you do, or call a backend. Your people, salaries, meetings and settings stay on your device.

No account
No cloud database
No analytics or tracking
No external APIs

Purchases are handled by Apple. Shared Team Cost Cards contain aggregate totals, never individual compensation.
